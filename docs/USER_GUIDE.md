# AutoSuite User & Administrator Guide

## Customer Journey
1. Browse available vehicles on `/`, `/fleet`, `/sales`, or `/rentals` using brand, category, transmission, fuel, and availability filters.
2. Open any vehicle detail page (`/vehicles/:id`) to inspect specs, features, and live rental pricing.
3. Sign in via `/auth` using your Google account.
4. Submit a rental booking request (with pickup/return locations, dates, and Self-Drive or Chauffeur mode) or a purchase/inspection inquiry.
5. Track booking statuses, contract references, recorded payments, outstanding balances, and favorites inside `/portal`.

## Administrator & Staff Journey
1. Sign in with an administrator account to access `/admin`.
2. On first launch, click **Seed Sample Showroom** in Dashboard Overview to populate starter brands, categories, and 6 studio-photographed vehicles, or add vehicles manually under **Vehicles**.
3. Approve pending rental requests under **Rental Bookings** (overlaps are automatically blocked inside atomic transactions), log pickup/return inspections, and generate printable contracts.
4. Record cash, bank transfer, or deposit payments under **Payments** to update booking balances in real time.
5. Configure your dealership's brand identity, operating mode, currency, and rental policies under **Business Settings**.
6. Export filtered inventory, booking, inquiry, customer, and payment CSV reports under **Reports**.
